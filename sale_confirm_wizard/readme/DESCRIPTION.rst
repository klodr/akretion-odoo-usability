When you confirm a quotation, Odoo opens a small wizard where you can check and update important information:

* customer PO number,
* delivery address,
* invoicing address,
* delivery date,
* payment terms.

It also displays the sale warning of the customer's company. If it is a blocking warning, the quotation cannot be confirmed.

This module has been developed because experience has shown that, when a sales assistant confirms a quotation, the important information written in the customer PO, which may differ from the quotation, is easily overlooked, which causes errors in delivery and invoicing.
